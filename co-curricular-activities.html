At Shree Mahalingeshwara Vidyanikethana, we believe that a child's education is not limited to the classroom. Co-curricular activities are an essential part of school life — giving every child the opportunity to explore their interests, discover hidden talents, and build confidence beyond academics.

From the stillness of daily yoga to the energy of sports, from classical dance to community service, our activities are thoughtfully designed around the school's Panchangashikshan framework — nurturing the physical, intellectual, cultural, creative, and moral growth of every student.

A World Beyond the Classroom

Every child at Shree Mahalingeshwara Vidyanikethana is encouraged to explore, create, and grow through a wide range of activities — from yoga and sports to music, dance, service, and the arts.

Well-being

Yoga & Meditation

Daily practice for physical and mental well-being — building stillness, focus, and inner calm that stays with the child for life.

Fitness

Sports & Athletics

Team spirit, fitness, and healthy competition — with active participation in inter-school and district-level competitions.

Expression

Music & Classical Arts

Vocal, instrumental, and classical music training — giving every child a joyful way to express emotion through sound and rhythm.

Culture

Bharatanatyam

Classical dance as a form of cultural expression — connecting students to India's artistic heritage with grace, discipline, and joy.

Discipline

Karate

Self-defence, discipline, and physical confidence — building strength of body, focus of mind, and respect for others.

Creativity

Craft & Art

Creativity, imagination, and hands-on skill — giving every child the freedom to make, shape, and see the world in their own way.

Dignity of Labour

Farming Exposure

Hands-on contact with farming and physical work — learning early that every form of honest effort deserves respect.

Heritage

Sanskrit & Heritage

Cultural roots and ancient wisdom — introducing students to Sanskrit shlokas, Indian traditions, and the values that shape a life well-lived.

Service

Community Service

Learning to serve the school, the village, and the wider world — with humility, care, and a heart for others.

Empowering young minds with knowledge, values and compassion for a brighter tomorrow.
