Academic Honors & Leadership

Scholarships, Excellence & Student Leadership

Recognizing scholastic brilliance, ethical character, student council leadership, and active involvement in eco-preservation and community clubs.

Meritorious students achieving over 90% in academic evaluations are rewarded with special merit scholarships, certificates of honour, and cash encouragement to support their continuing academic journey.
