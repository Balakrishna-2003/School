What We Do Together

A Team That Grows Together

From festive celebrations to training sessions and parent meetings, our staff come together throughout the year to learn, bond and serve the school community.

Team Building

Festivals such as Onam and Teachers' Day bring every teacher and staff member together, strengthening friendships and a shared sense of purpose.

Professional Growth

Teachers take the stage to speak, present and lead programmes, building confidence and sharing good practices with colleagues.

Community Events

PTA meetings, inaugurations and cultural programmes keep staff, parents and management working hand in hand.
