Primary Clubs Directory

Every student from Primary to High School is encouraged to be an active member of at least one club, guided by dedicated faculty mentors and specialized trainers.

Arts & Crafts
Srujana Kala Sangha

Creative Arts & Crafts Club

A haven for young artists to explore varied mediums including oil pastels, origami, greeting card design, paper crafts, and traditional floral art.

  • 3D Paper craft, origami & lotus flower modeling
  • Sketching, watercolor blending & canvas painting
  • Festive rangoli design & school wall art exhibitions
Fridays 2:30 PM Grades 1 – 10
Science & STEM
Vignana & Ganitha Sangha

Science & Innovation Club

Encouraging hands-on scientific discovery, circuit wiring, microscopy, chemical reactions, and mathematical problem-solving workshops.

  • Practical Physics circuit & optical experiments
  • Biology specimen observation under compound microscope
  • District Science Fair working model development
Wednesdays 3:00 PM Grades 4 – 10
Performing Arts
Sangeetha & Nrithya Kalakshetra

Music & Dance Guild

Rooting students in India's rich cultural heritage through classical Kathak, Bharatanatyam, regional folk dances, and Carnatic vocal training.

  • Kathak rhythm footwork & Bharatanatyam mudras
  • Carnatic classical vocal, Bhajans & Harmonium
  • Kalotsavam & Annual Cultural stage ensembles
Tuesdays & Fridays Grades 1 – 10
Sports & Martial Arts
Kreeda & Sahasa Sangha

Martial Arts & Sports Guild

Empowering students with disciplined Karate self-defense training under certified Sensei, quadrangle roller skating, and athletics conditioning.

  • Certified Karate belt gradings, katas & kumite
  • Speed roller skating drills & courtyard balance
  • Athletic sprint track training & Annual Sports Meet
Daily Morning & Friday Grades 1 – 10
Eco & Nature
Prakrithi Parisara Samiti

Eco & Nature Club

Instilling environmental consciousness through campus herbal gardening, Vanamahotsava tree plantation drives, and eco-craft exhibitions.

  • World Environment Day sapling plantation
  • Campus herbal garden nurture & composting awareness
  • Botanical butterfly & recycled nature craft projects
Thursdays 3:15 PM Grades 3 – 10
Literary & Drama
Sahitya, Natya & Samskritha Vedike

Literary & Theatrical Club

Nurturing multi-lingual eloquence, Sanskrit Gita shloka recitation, debate skills, and mythological theatrical drama stage productions.

  • Epic Ramayana & historical theatrical drama
  • Sanskrit Shloka chanting & Gita recitation contests
  • Hindi Diwas & Kannada Rajyotsava debate circles
Mondays 2:30 PM Grades 3 – 10
Campus Activities

Special Activities & Skill Training

Dedicated professional training and enrichment programs operating within the school campus to provide students with holistic physical, martial, and cultural development.

Martial Arts Activity

Karate & Self-Defense Training

Conducted by certified Black Belt Sensei instructors, our martial arts activity trains students in Shotokan Karate fundamentals, discipline, kata forms, and practical self-defense techniques.

  • Official belt promotion exams & grading certificates
  • State & District Karate Championship participation
  • Specialized self-defense workshops for girls
Sensei Supervised
Speed & Balance Activity

Courtyard Roller Skating Activity

Featuring smooth tiled open quadrangle rinks, professional quad and inline skate coaching helps students master balance, speed control, slalom cones, and endurance.

  • Quad & Inline professional skating techniques
  • Full safety gear & certified trainer supervision
  • Intra-school speed trials & sports day display
Speed & Agility
Performing Arts Activity

Classical Dance & Heritage Troupe

Under expert Gurus, students undergo systematic practice in Kathak, Bharatanatyam, and traditional folk dances, adorning authentic costumes and performing at prestigious cultural festivals.

  • Rigorous Abhinaya, Bhavas & Taal rhythmic mastery
  • District & State Kalotsavam Gold Medal achievements
  • Grand Annual Day themed cultural theatricals
State Awardees
Holistic Wellness Activity

Daily Yoga & Mindful Meditation

Integrated into the daily morning routine, students practice 12-step Surya Namaskars, Pranayama breath control, and concentration meditation in the breezy campus quadrangle.

  • International Yoga Day mass synchronous displays
  • Enhanced focus, postural balance & mental clarity
  • Breathing exercises for stress-free exam prep
Daily Practice
Democratic Spirit

Student Leadership & Club Governance

Clubs at Shree Mahalingeshwara Vidyanikethana are driven by elected student secretaries under the guidance of faculty advisors, empowering children to plan, coordinate, and lead real-world events.

Democratic Elections

Students elect Head Boy, Head Girl, and House Captains through secret ballot voting.

Club Secretaries

Each club elects student secretaries to manage attendance, event planning, and logs.

Investiture Ceremony

Sashes and badges are awarded with solemn oaths to uphold integrity and service.

Community Outreach

Student leaders organize Kargil Vijay Diwas tributes, environmental rallies, and health visits.
