Founder's Message

A vision rooted in
truth and service

The Shree Mahalingeshwara Temple Education Trust was founded on a simple belief — that every child deserves the freedom to learn, to grow, and to live in truth.

What began as a small act of service on the temple grounds of Kunjathur has grown into a school where knowledge and compassion walk together.

Shree Mahalingeshwara Temple Education Trust

Founder

When we first thought of starting a school, we did not begin with a building or a syllabus. We began with a question — what kind of place would allow a child to grow without fear, without pressure, and without losing the natural curiosity they are born with?

The answer took shape as the Shree Mahalingeshwara Temple Education Trust — a non-profit body on the temple grounds in Kunjathur, created to serve the children of our community. From the very beginning, we held on to the belief that education must be rooted in "living in truth." Everything we do today — from the NCERT-guided curriculum to daily meditation, from physical activity and music to the arts — flows from that one principle.

We did not want our school to be measured only by marks. We wanted it to be measured by the kind of human beings it helps shape — young people who are capable, responsible, and deeply rooted in Bharat's cultural and philosophical heritage. This is why our curriculum draws not only from textbooks, but also from India's ancient traditions, its cultural legacy, and the wisdom of respected teachers worldwide. It is why we introduce students to lesser-known figures from Indian history, and why we give them hands-on exposure to farming and the dignity of labour.

Perhaps the most important decision we made was this — that no child should be turned away because of money. A midday meal is served to everyone on campus, funded through voluntary contributions from parents and supporters. Fees are kept minimal, and waived entirely for those who cannot afford to pay. In doing so, we hope our students learn a quiet lesson that no textbook can fully teach — that knowledge and prosperity should ultimately serve the wider community.

Our greatest pride is our teaching staff. They are trained to interact with children in a way that preserves their inner freedom, often meditating alongside their students. Each teacher is trusted with the kind of freedom a mother has — the freedom to care, to nurture, and to guide.

To the parents who have placed their trust in us, and to the children who come to us each morning — thank you. This school exists because of you. And it will continue to grow as long as we hold on to the simple intention with which we began.
