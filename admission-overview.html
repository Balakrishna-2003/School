Eligibility Rules

Age Criteria

Please review the age requirement before applying, to make sure your child is eligible for the class you wish to enrol them in.

Grade 1 Admission

6 Years Completed as on 31st March

To be admitted to Grade 1, the child must have completed 6 years of age as on 31st March of the admission year. This ensures they're ready to begin their formal schooling journey with confidence.

Application Steps

Two Ways to Apply

You can submit your admission application either directly at the school office or online — whichever is more convenient for you.

01

Apply in Person

Visit the school office and collect the prescribed admission form. Fill it out and submit it directly along with the required documents.

At School Office
02

Apply Online

Complete the admission form online at your convenience, then submit it with the required documents and the online payment fee challan.

From Anywhere
Mark Your Calendar

Important Dates

Admissions for the next academic year follow a clear yearly cycle — here are the dates you should keep in mind.

Admissions Open

Every January

Admissions open every January for the next academic year. Parents are encouraged to apply early, as seats are limited and offered on a first-come basis.

Last Date to Apply

15th May

The last date to submit the filled application along with the online payment fee challan is 15th May every year.

Need help with your application?
