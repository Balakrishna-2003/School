Shree Mahalingeshwara Vidyanikethana, Kunjathur is a school operated by the Shree Mahalingeshwara Temple Education Trust, a registered charitable body based in Kunjathur, Kerala. The trust was set up by a group of individuals and functions under the guidance of the Shree Mahalingeshwara Temple.

PRINCIPAL'S MESSAGE

Nurturing Young Minds for a Brighter Tomorrow

Education is not merely about academic success; it is about nurturing responsible, confident and compassionate individuals.

At our school, we strive to provide a learning environment where every child feels valued, motivated and inspired to discover their potential. Together, with the support of parents, teachers and the community, we aim to shape responsible citizens who contribute positively to society.
