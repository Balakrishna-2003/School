President's Message

Guiding with purpose,
serving with integrity

As President of the Shree Mahalingeshwara Temple Education Trust, it is my privilege to lead an institution founded on service, rooted in tradition, and committed to the future of every child who walks through our doors.

Our school is more than a place of learning — it is a community built on trust, where knowledge and compassion walk together, and where every decision is guided by what is best for the child.

Suresh Kumar Shetty, Balaka

President, Shree Mahalingeshwara Temple Education Trust

When I look at our school today, I see something far beyond bricks and classrooms. I see a promise — a promise made by the Trust to the families of Kunjathur, that their children would have a place to learn that is safe, dignified, and rooted in values.

The Shree Mahalingeshwara Temple Education Trust was established with the belief that education should not be a privilege of the few, but a right shared by all. Every policy we frame, every rupee we spend, and every hour our teachers give is guided by this single conviction.

Our affiliation with NIOS, New Delhi, and our continued commitment to the NCERT-guided curriculum allow us to offer a modern, quality education. But we have never let the syllabus overshadow something more important — the formation of sound character. From daily meditation to physical activity, from music and the arts to service and farming, our children learn not only how to succeed, but how to live with purpose.

One of the decisions I am most proud of is that no child is ever turned away for want of money. Our midday meal is served to everyone on campus — students, staff, and guests. Fees are kept minimal and waived where needed. In doing so, we hope every child carries forward the belief that prosperity is meaningful only when it serves others.

To our teachers, I extend my deepest gratitude. You are the heart of this institution, and the trust we place in you is the trust of a parent. To our parents and supporters, I thank you for walking with us. And to our students — you are the reason we exist. Every day you remind us why this work matters.

As we look ahead, the Trust remains committed to growing this school thoughtfully and steadily — always keeping the child at the centre, always honouring the values on which we were founded. This is our promise, and we renew it today.

Suresh Kumar Shetty, Balaka

President, Shree Mahalingeshwara Temple Education Trust

Empowering young minds with knowledge, values and compassion for a brighter tomorrow.
