Curriculum Architecture

A Progressive Pathway from Foundations to Mastery

An integrated academic journey spanning Primary and Middle School years — cultivating curiosity, trilingual fluency, practical numeracy, scientific rigour, and enduring character excellence.

Primary Classes

Classes I to IV

A balanced curriculum that nurtures curiosity, creativity and good values, while building strong academic foundations.

01
Language

English

Foundational Core

Reading, writing, comprehension, and confident oral communication — building a strong linguistic base from the very first year.

02
Regional Language

Malayalam / Kannada

Mother Tongue

Reading, writing, and conversation in the child's regional language — connecting them deeply to their linguistic heritage and regional culture.

03
National Language

Hindi

Early Acquisition

Introduction to the national language — phonetics, simple vocabulary, basic reading, and daily conversational skills.

04
Numbers & Reasoning

Mathematics

Numeracy & Logic

Numbers, arithmetic, shapes, patterns, and mental math puzzles — developing numerical confidence through practical real-life examples.

05
Nature & Science

Environmental Studies (EVS)

Experiential

A first look at the natural world — plants, animals, seasons, and local ecosystems, taught through observation and outdoor activities.

06
Digital Skills

Computer Education

Digital Literacy

Introduction to computers, mouse & keyboard skills, educational tools, and safe digital exploration — early technical readiness.

07
Creativity

Art Education

Fine Arts & Craft

Drawing, colouring, craft, and origami — stimulating fine motor skills, imagination, and joyful aesthetic self-expression.

08
Everyday Wisdom

Life Skills

Emotional Balance

Practical habits for daily life — personal hygiene, sharing, safety awareness, empathy, and positive decision-making exercises.

09
Moral Foundation

Value Education

Character Building

Storytelling from Indian heritage, moral fables, respect for elders, and core human values that quietly shape character.

10
Health & Fitness

Physical Education & Yoga

Sports & Yoga

Yoga asanas, motor coordination drills, team sports, and outdoor physical play — building stamina and disciplined vitality.

11
World Awareness

General Knowledge

Broad Horizons

A window to the wider world — current events, national symbols, universe facts, nature wonders, and inspiring personalities.

12
Cultural Arts

Music & Chanting

Rhythm & Vocal

Devotional songs, shlokas, patriotic melodies, and rhythmic singing — nurturing emotional harmony and cultural heritage.

Middle School

Classes V to VIII

An advanced curriculum advancing into analytical inquiry, specialized science disciplines, third language depth, and higher academic rigour.

01
Language & Literature

English

Advanced Literature

Advanced reading, grammar, composition, and literature — guiding students into analytical appreciation and articulate, persuasive writing.

02
National Language

Hindi

Grammar & Prose

Deeper reading, advanced grammar, and literary prose in Hindi — strengthening fluency and analytical command over the national language.

03
Elective Choice

Third Language

Malayalam · Kannada · Sanskrit

A customized choice between regional languages (Malayalam or Kannada) or classical Sanskrit — broadening linguistic range and cultural depth.

04
Algebra · Geometry

Mathematics

Analytical Rigour

Algebra, geometry, data handling, and structured problem-solving — building the analytical rigour needed for secondary classes.

05
Physics · Chem · Bio

Science

Lab & Inquiry

Rigorous introduction to the three scientific branches — with experimental demonstrations, inquiry-led investigation, and concept clarity.

06
History · Geo · Civics

Social Science

Civic Awareness

Indian history, physical geography, and democratic civic life — cultivating historical perspective and responsible citizenship.

07
Ethics & Integrity

Value Education

Character

Ethics, moral reasoning, empathy, and personal integrity — continuing to shape thoughtful, ethical leaders with deep human values.

08
Practical Wisdom

Life Skills

Leadership

Communication, teamwork, critical thinking, time management, and emotional resilience — preparing students for higher studies.

09
Computer Science

Work Experience

Applied Computing

Hands-on computing, digital tools, coding fundamentals, and practical projects — cultivating early technical readiness.

Guiding Educational Philosophy
“Education is the manifestation of the perfection already in man.”
— Swami Vivekananda • Awakening wisdom, noble character, and lifelong curiosity in every student
Class Structure

The Full Academic Path

From LKG through Grade 8, a single unbroken path — with each stage preparing the child for the one that follows.

LKG · UKG

Pre-Primary

Play-based learning — early phonics, numeracy, motor skills, and social confidence in a warm, joyful environment.

Classes 1 – 4

Primary Stage

Foundational trilingual literacy (English, Kannada, Hindi), practical numeracy, environmental awareness, and creative character building.

Classes 5 – 8

Middle School

Deeper sciences (Physics, Chemistry, Biology), advanced mathematics, third language choice, and structured preparation for higher studies.
