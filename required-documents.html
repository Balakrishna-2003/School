Documents Required

Please bring the following documents (original and one set of photocopies) when visiting the school office for admission.

Category 01

Student Documents

  • Birth Certificate Original with photocopy
  • Aadhaar Card Student's copy
  • Passport-Size Photographs Recent, 4 copies
  • Transfer Certificate If applicable
Category 02

Parent / Guardian

  • Aadhaar Card Father and mother
  • Passport-Size Photographs Parents, 2 copies each
  • Contact Details Mobile number & email
  • Address Proof Ration card / utility bill
Category 03

Academic Records

  • Previous Report Card Last class passed
  • Transfer Certificate From previous school
  • Conduct Certificate If applicable
  • Migration Certificate For higher classes
Category 04

Additional Documents

  • Caste Certificate If applicable
  • Medical Record Vaccination / health card
  • Income Certificate For scholarship applicants
  • Any Other Documents As advised by school office
How It Works

Admission Process

A simple four-step process to complete your child's admission at Shree Mahalingeshwara Vidyanikethana.

01

Fill Enquiry Form

Submit an admission enquiry through our online form or visit the school office.

02

Submit Documents

Bring all required documents (original + photocopies) for verification.

03

Pay Admission Fee

Complete the payment at the school office as per the approved fee structure.

04

Confirm Admission

Receive the confirmation and welcome kit — your child is now part of our family!
